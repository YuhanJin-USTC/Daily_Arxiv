To make the $\ell_{p/2}$'s the same, the lines started on the small major radius side of the plasma must undergo wiggles to make their $\ell_{p/2}$ as long as those started on the outboard side. This is achievable either using modular coils with a large helical component on the small major radius side or with a central column carrying a helical current.
