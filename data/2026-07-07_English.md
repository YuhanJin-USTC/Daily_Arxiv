It is then applied to the RF cavity of the Storage-Ring-based Coherent Light Source (SRCLS), where the extracted HOM parameters are used to reconstruct total impedance spectra, evaluate coupled-bunch instability thresholds, and guide cavity-geometry optimization. The results demonstrate an efficient connection between wakefield analysis, eigenmode characterization, and beam-stability evaluation for practical RF-cavity designs.
