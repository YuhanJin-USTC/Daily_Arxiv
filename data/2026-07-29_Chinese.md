Motivation: 激光驱动X射线源存在显著脉冲波动，需要校准的探测器响应模型来实现光子通量和光谱分布的定量测量。闪烁体平板探测器虽已广泛应用于激光等离子体X射线源诊断，但其定量校准尚不充分。

Method: 利用ISO 4037 N系列标准参考辐射品质（受控多色X射线场），对CsI:Tl平板探测器和塑料闪烁体滤波堆栈光谱仪两种互补型探测器系统进行了信号响应和信号-通量转换关系的标定实验。

Result: 建立了两种探测器在标准参考辐射下的信号响应特性和信号-通量转换系数，为激光驱动X射线源的定量测量提供了校准依据。

Conclusion: 通过ISO标准辐射品质校准，CsI:Tl平板探测器和塑料闪烁体滤波堆栈光谱仪可用于激光等离子体X射线源的定量诊断，实现光子通量和光谱分布的可靠测量。

Abstract: Laser-driven X-ray sources produce broadband radiation with substantial shot-to-shot fluctuations, requiring calibrated detector-response models for quantitative measurements of photon fluence and spectral distribution. Scintillator-based flat-panel detectors, originally developed primarily for medical and industrial X-ray imaging, are increasingly being adopted for diagnostics of laser-plasma-based X-ray sources because they provide large-area, spatially resolved detection. We report the calibration of two complementary X-ray detector systems: a CsI:Tl-based flat-panel detector and a plastic-scintillator filter-stack spectrometer intended for spectral reconstruction. Both detectors were characterized using well-defined ISO 4037 N-series reference radiation qualities, providing controlled polychromatic X-ray fields for establishing their signal response and signal-to-fluence conversion.
