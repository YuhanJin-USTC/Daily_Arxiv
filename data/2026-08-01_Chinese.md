Result: 成功实现了首个汞原子光学偶极阱，完成了六种Hg同位素的转移，并表征了阱的关键参数（装载动力学、阱深、寿命）。

Conclusion: 该工作为超冷Hg的研究开辟了新途径：有望用于同位素依赖的碰撞和光缔合研究，并为实现量子简并提供了可行路线。

Abstract: We report the first realization of an optical dipole trap (ODT) for ultracold mercury atoms, overcoming the challenge posed by the exceptionally low polarizability of Hg and the resulting weak optical trapping potential. We demonstrate the transfer of six naturally abundant Hg isotopes from a magneto-optical trap (MOT) into the ODT and characterize its loading dynamics, trap depth, and lifetime. Confinement of dense samples of ultracold Hg opens new opportunities for studies of isotope-dependent collisions and photoassociation and provides a route toward quantum degeneracy.
