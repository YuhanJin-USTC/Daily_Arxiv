Stationary parallel heat fluxes up to $100\,\text{MW m}^{-2}$ are measured at the divertor target, an order of magnitude above previous TCV power exhaust studies. The obtained SOL collisionality and Lengyel detachment scaling metric lie within range of values expected in future reactors (SPARC, ITER, ARC).
