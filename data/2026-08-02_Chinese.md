Motivation: 宽视场成像与高空间分辨率之间存在根本性权衡：宽视场相机因像素有限而牺牲采样密度，长焦系统则受限于场景覆盖范围。传统光束偏转器件难以同时满足全固态、大口径和高速度的要求，亟需一种能够突破这一权衡的成像架构。

Method: 提出级联液晶光束偏转（CaLiBS）成像系统，包含级联液晶波片和液晶Pancharatnam-Berry相位光栅；开发了理论电压预测模型结合层次化搜索算法，在斜入射条件下实现高效标定，标定速度比传统方法快10倍。系统在30.3°×30.3°范围内以2°间隔偏转子视场，衍射效率超过60%，通过顺序子视场采集重建复合图像，并结合目标跟踪方法实现运动车辆的高分辨率跟踪。

Result: 标定后的系统在30.3°×30.3°、2°间隔的子视场偏转中衍射效率高于60%；顺序子视场采集重建了34.7°×34.7°的复合图像，与使用相同探测器的单次宽视场相机相比，空间带宽积提升了8.6倍；结合目标跟踪方法，实现了广域场景内运动车辆的高分辨率跟踪。

Conclusion: 级联液晶架构提供了一条可扩展的路径，可实现紧凑、无振动且高分辨率的宽视场观测，为宽视场高分辨率成像技术提供了一种有前景的解决方案。

Abstract: High-resolution wide-field imaging is essential for applications requiring simultaneous global coverage and local detail, yet conventional approaches face a fundamental trade-off: wide-FOV cameras sacrifice spatial sampling density by distributing finite detector pixels over a broad angular range, while telephoto systems resolve fine features at the cost of scene coverage. Beam-steering devices can mitigate this trade-off but are currently limited in achieving simultaneously all-solid-state, large aperture, and high-speed operation. Here, we report an all-solid-state large-aperture cascaded liquid-crystal beam-steering (CaLiBS) imaging system that extends the effective angular range of a high-resolution narrow-FOV camera by electrically steering sub-FOVs. The CaLiBS module comprises cascaded liquid crystal waveplates and liquid crystal Pancharatnam-Berry phase gratings; a theoretical voltage-prediction model with a hierarchical search algorithm enables efficient calibration under oblique incidence and 10 times faster calibration speed compared with conventional methods. The calibrated system addresses sub-FOVs across 30.3° * 30.3° at 2° intervals with diffraction efficiency above 60%. Sequential sub-FOV acquisition reconstructs a 34.7 * 34.7 composite image, an 8.6-fold enhancement in spatial-bandwidth product over a single-shot wide-FOV camera using the same detector. Combined with object tracking methods, sub-FOV switching further enables high-resolution tracking of moving vehicles within the wide-area scene. This cascaded LC architecture offers a scalable pathway toward compact, vibration-free, and high-resolution wide-field observation.
