Motivation: 压电致动器在超导射频腔中至关重要，用于补偿洛伦兹力失谐。然而在低温下压电行程会减小，现有测量方法要么使用SRF腔本身作为传感器，要么依赖电容测量，这些方法都不直接测量行程，且在测量精度与实验简便性、成本和时间之间存在权衡。

Method: 开发了一种新颖的直接测量方法，在低温恒温器内部使用激光位移传感器，在真空和低温条件下直接测量压电致动器的行程。该方法应用于KEK正在建造的ILC原型低温模块的腔频率调谐器压电致动器的表征和评估。

Result: 成功开发并测试了这种新型测量方法，能够在真空和低温条件下直接测量压电致动器的行程。该方法克服了传统方法的局限性，提供了更精确、直接的测量手段。

Conclusion: 该研究提出了一种创新的直接测量方法，能够在低温条件下精确测量压电致动器的行程，为超导射频腔压电致动器的性能评估提供了更可靠的技术手段，对ILC原型低温模块的开发具有重要意义。

Abstract: Piezoelectric actuators are critical for achieving high accelerating gradients and preventing RF trips in narrow-bandwidth superconducting radio-frequency (SRF) cavities by compensating for detuning caused by Lorentz force detuning. Depending on the maximum acceleration gradient an appropriate piezo stroke requirement has to be fulfilled. Since the stroke of piezo actuators decreases at cryogenic temperatures, evaluating their performance under such conditions is essential. Common characterization methods either use the SRF cavity itself as a sensor or rely on capacitance measurements during cool-down. Both these approaches do not measure the stroke directly and involve a trade-off between measurement precision and experimental simplicity, as well as cost and time. We developed a new method for the direct and precise measurement of piezo stroke at cryogenic temperature inside a cryocooler-cooled cryostat using a laser displacement sensor. The setup was used to characterize and evaluate two piezo actuators for cavity frequency tuners of the ILC prototype cryomodule, which is currently being built at KEK. In this article we are reporting on the development, setup, test, and application of this novel method, allowing the direct stroke measurement of piezos in vacuum and at cryogenic temperatures.
