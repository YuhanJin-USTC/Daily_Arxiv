Main category: physics.comp-ph

TL;DR: 开发了一个两步骤框架，通过简化化学模型量化详细化学动力学模型参数引入的不确定性，应用于燃烧设备模拟中，显著降低了计算成本。

Motivation: 在复杂燃烧设备的高保真数值模拟中，化学反应速率参数引入的不确定性传播对于评估计算预测的影响至关重要。然而，详细计算的高成本加上需要进行多次模拟来传播不确定性，使得这种估计在计算上具有挑战性。

Method: 开发了一个两步骤框架：首先，通过沿着未燃混合状态轨迹积分到规定的进程变量约束，在完整组成空间中唯一重建简化流形状态；其次，通过从机理协方差矩阵采样扰动速率系数并将每个实现积分到目标状态，传播参数不确定性，生成简化空间量的不确定性图。

Result: 该方法应用于两种配置：具有相互作用射流火焰和再循环的亚音速多管燃烧器，以及三维反应高速流道。报告了轨迹时间（重建的未反应混合物达到局部目标状态所需时间）和达到平衡时间的仪器化不确定性估计，揭示了由混合、分层和停留时间效应驱动的数量级空间变化。最大的相对变异性发生在与诱导和热释放开始相关的低到中等温度区域，其中分支相关化学放大了敏感性，特别是在非化学计量条件下。

Conclusion: 该方法为实际反应流模拟提供了可扩展的、空间分辨的、物理可解释的化学不确定性量化途径，能够有效降低计算成本并揭示关键化学敏感性区域。

Abstract: Propagating uncertainties introduced by chemical reaction rate parameters to high-fidelity numerical simulations of complex combustion devices is necessary to ascertain impact on computational predictions. However, the high cost of detailed computations combined with the need to conduct multiple simulations to propagate uncertainty makes such an estimation computationally challenging. In order to reduce the computational cost, a two-step framework for quantifying uncertainty introduced by detailed chemical kinetics model parameters using reduced chemistry models is developed here. First, reduced-manifold states are uniquely reconstructed in full-composition space by following trajectories at an unburnt mixing state and integrating forward to a prescribed progress variable constraint. Second, parametric uncertainty is propagated by sampling perturbed rate coefficients from mechanism covariance matrices and integrating each realization to the target state, yielding uncertainty maps for reduced-space quantities. The method is applied in two configurations: a subsonic multi-tube combustor with interacting jet flames and recirculation, and a three-dimensional reacting high-speed flowpath. Uncertainty-instrumented estimated are reported for a trajectory time (time for the reconstructed unreacted mixture to reach the local target state) and for the time to equilibrium, revealing order-of-magnitude spatial variations driven by mixing, stratification, and residence-time effects. The largest relative variability occurs in low-to-intermediate temperature regimes associated with induction and the onset of heat release, where branching-related chemistry amplifies sensitivity, particularly away from stoichiometric conditions. The method provides a scalable route to spatially resolved, physically interpretable chemistry-UQ for practical reacting-flow simulations.
