Abstract: We examine the consistency of the equilibrium magnetic field $\mathbf{B}_0$ used in the analytical calculation of the sideways wall force by A. A. Martynov and S. Yu. Medvedev [Phys. In these attempts, the current density $\mathbf{j}_0 = \nabla \times \mathbf{B}_0$ must vanish in the vacuum regions adjacent to the wall, making $\mathbf{B}_0$ curl-free as a complement to the natural $\nabla \cdot \mathbf{B}_0 = 0$.
