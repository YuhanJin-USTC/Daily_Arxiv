### [1] [Kinetic Fluctuation-Dissipation Relation in collisionless plasmas: a correlated noise source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29709)
*Fabio Moretti, Oreste Pezzi, Simone Benella, Giuseppe Consolini, Andrea Larosa, Fabio Lepreti, Luca Sorriso-Valvo, Francesco Valentini, Vincenzo Carbone*

Main category: physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: The paper develops a generalized kinetic Langevin framework with stochastic time-correlated forcing to study small-scale collisionless plasma dynamics, derives steady-state fluctuation-dissipation relations and spectral scalings, characterizes velocity-space cascades via Hermite decomposition, extends the model to power-law-distributed multi-scale forcing rates, and validates the analytical predictions with direct numerical integrations.

Motivation: The study is motivated by the need to understand small-scale dynamics of collisionless plasmas, including energy injection, energy conversion, spectral behavior, and velocity-space cascades, and to provide closed-form expressions that may help interpret spacecraft in-situ observations.

Method: The authors employ a generalized kinetic Langevin framework driven by stochastic time-correlated forcing. They analyze a steady-state Fluctuation-Dissipation Relation in which low-frequency energy injection comes from the forcing and small-scale energy conversion occurs via Landau damping as an effective dissipation mechanism. They derive asymptotic spectral scalings analytically, decompose the plasma distribution function with Hermite polynomials to characterize the velocity-space cascade, and generalize the framework to multi-scale forcing with correlation rates distributed according to a power law. Analytical predictions are tested through direct numerical integrations of the underlying kinetic equations.

Result: The framework captures a steady-state Fluctuation-Dissipation Relation, yields asymptotic spectral scalings across multiple physical regimes, and characterizes the velocity-space cascade through Hermite decomposition. The multi-scale forcing extension provides closed-form analytical expressions intended to aid interpretation of spacecraft in-situ observations. All analytical predictions are reported to be successfully validated by direct numerical integrations of the underlying kinetic equations.

Conclusion: The generalized kinetic Langevin framework provides analytically tractable and numerically validated descriptions of small-scale collisionless plasma dynamics, including effective Landau-damping-mediated energy conversion, spectral scalings, velocity-space cascades, and multi-scale forcing effects, with potential utility for interpreting spacecraft observations.

Abstract: We here focus on small-scale dynamics of collisionless plasmas by employing a generalized kinetic Langevin framework driven by a stochastic time-correlated forcing. Our investigation captures the steady-state Fluctuation-Dissipation Relation (FDR) where the energy injection at low frequencies is due to the time-correlated forcing and the small-scale energy conversion occurs via Landau damping, the latter --though formally reversible-- acting as an effective dissipation mechanism. We analytically extract the resulting asymptotic spectral scalings across multiple physical regimes and characterize the velocity-space cascade by decomposing the plasma distribution function with Hermite polynomials. The framework is then generalized to a multi-scale forcing scenario in which correlation rates are distributed according to a power-law distribution. This extension provides closed-form analytical expressions that may serve to interpret spacecraft in-situ observations. All analytical predictions are successfully validated through direct numerical integrations of the underlying kinetic equations.
