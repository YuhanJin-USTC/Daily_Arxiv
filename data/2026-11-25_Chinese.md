TL;DR: 该文提出一种将鳍片内声子玻尔兹曼输运方程的预训练神经算子与共享衬底傅里叶模型耦合的混合框架，在100个硅鳍阵列上以约180倍加速复现直接BTE-Fourier参考结果，最大温差0.3 K、相对热流误差0.6%。

研究动机： 半导体器件中的局部加热会在热源附近产生非扩散声子输运，而周围衬底的热扩散仍可由傅里叶传导较好描述；直接进行BTE-Fourier双向耦合计算成本高，尤其在多个鳍片位置和耦合迭代中重复求解BTE时。

方法： 构建鳍片内声子BTE神经算子与衬底傅里叶模型的混合耦合框架；用DeepONet将每个鳍片的衬底界面温度和加热状态映射到其温度场与热流场，以减少重复BTE求解；训练数据来自耦合问题线性化代理模型生成的界面温度，避免从完整耦合运行中显式采集；采用能量平衡惩罚正则化学习算子；衬底热流用H(div)协调空间的混合有限元表示；两个模型通过非重叠Schwarz迭代交换温度和法向热流，并使用自适应Aitken松弛。

结果： 在由100个硅鳍组成的理想化三维阵列上评估；对一个留出开关配置，神经算子-Fourier解与直接BTE-Fourier参考相比，所有鳍片的最大温度差为0.3 K，最大相对热流误差为0.6%；不计一次性衬底分解，耦合代理运行约50 s，而直接耦合需2 h 28 min，加速约180倍；其他留出开关模式也能用同一训练算子收敛。

结论： 结果表明，神经算子可加速所测试几何与材料下的双向动力学-连续介质耦合；该双向策略有望扩展到其他将学习型局部神经模型与连续介质求解器耦合的多尺度材料问题。

原始摘要： Localized heating in semiconductor devices can produce non-diffusive phonon transport near heat-generating regions, while heat spreading through the surrounding substrate remains well described by Fourier conduction. We present a hybrid framework that couples a pretrained neural operator for the phonon Boltzmann transport equation (BTE) within individual fins to a Fourier model of a shared substrate. To reduce the cost of repeated BTE solves across fin positions and coupling iterations, a DeepONet maps each fin's substrate-interface temperature and heating state to its temperature and heat-flux fields. The operator is trained on interface temperatures generated by a linearized proxy of the coupled problem, which avoids explicit training data collection from full coupled runs. An energy-balance penalty regularizes the learned operator, and a mixed finite-element formulation represents substrate heat flux in an H(div)-conforming space. The two models exchange temperature and normal heat flux through a non-overlapping Schwarz iteration with adaptive Aitken relaxation. We evaluate the framework on an idealized three-dimensional array of 100 silicon fins. For a held-out switching configuration, the neural-operator-Fourier solution differs from the direct BTE-Fourier reference by a maximum of 0.3 K in temperature and 0.6% in relative flux across all fins. Excluding the one-time substrate factorization, the coupled surrogate run takes about 50 s, compared with 2 h 28 min for direct coupling, a speedup of approximately 180x. Additional held-out switching patterns converge with the same trained operator. These results demonstrate neural-operator acceleration of bidirectional kinetic-continuum coupling for the tested geometry and material. This bidirectional strategy may extend to other multiscale materials problems coupling learned local neural models with continuum solvers.
