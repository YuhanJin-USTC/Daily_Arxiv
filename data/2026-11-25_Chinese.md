### [1] [Whistler instability driven by electron trapping in magnetic dips](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32922)
*Ida Svenningsson, István Pusztai, Konrad Steinvall, Tünde Fülöp*

分类： physics.plasm-ph

TL;DR: 该研究利用全动理学粒子模拟表明，离子尺度不稳定性释放的自由能可通过改变电子分布传递至电子尺度，并最终由磁坑中电子分布形状驱动的哨声波散射电子、沿磁场加热电子。


<details>
  <summary>详细分析</summary>
研究动机： 研究离子尺度不稳定性释放的自由能如何通过其对电子分布的影响传递到电子尺度。

方法： 采用全动理学粒子模拟（particle-in-cell simulations），并追踪速度空间中的能量传递。

结果： 初始离子温度各向异性驱动部分压缩性的离子回旋波并形成磁坑；被困电子形成增强的温度各向异性和蝴蝶形分布，类似地球磁鞘中的航天器观测。俘获造成的梯度通过回旋共振驱动哨声波，哨声波再散射电子俯仰角、抹平速度空间梯度并沿磁场加热电子；当哨声波功率达到峰值时，电子各向异性降至双绝热预测约一半。

原始摘要： Using fully kinetic particle-in-cell simulations, we show how free energy released by an ion-scale instability reaches electron scales through its effect on the electron distribution. An initial ion temperature anisotropy drives partly compressional ion cyclotron waves, which form magnetic dips. Electrons trapped in these dips develop an enhanced temperature anisotropy and butterfly-shaped distributions, similar to those observed by spacecraft in the Earth's magnetosheath. By tracking the energy transfer in velocity space, we show that the gradients created by trapping drive whistler waves through cyclotron resonance. The whistlers in turn scatter the electrons in pitch angle, flattening velocity-space gradients and heating the electrons along the magnetic field. As a result, the electron anisotropy drops to about half of the double-adiabatic prediction once the whistler power peaks. Our results show that it is the shape of the electron distribution in magnetic dips, rather than its temperature anisotropy alone, that drives the whistlers.
