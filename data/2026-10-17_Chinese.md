Applied to an unconfined Boussinesq plume and a viscous Burgers problem spanning a smooth-to-shock transition, CR-OpInf remains bounded for five training horizons, avoids the divergence of affine and unconstrained variants, tracks the plume rise, and recovers stratification-dependent rise scaling at moderate horizons. To our knowledge, this is the first parametric OpInf study of an unconfined stratified plume.
